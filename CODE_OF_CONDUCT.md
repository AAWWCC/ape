# Code of conduct

APE contributors must keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and free from harassment.
Disagreement about code, evidence, or product direction is welcome when it addresses the work and
not the person. Do not publish private information, use sexualized or discriminatory language, or
engage in threats, intimidation, or sustained disruption.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact path in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Maintainers
may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Reports will be
reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers, review threads, and
other spaces where someone represents APE.
